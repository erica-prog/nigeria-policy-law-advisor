# Nigeria: authoritative sources

Last updated 2026-10-24. **Not yet reviewed by a Nigerian-qualified lawyer.** Identify the
level (federal or state), the court or regulator, and the material date before relying on
anything below. Nigeria is a federation: federal legislation, state legislation and the
rules of each court are separate bodies of law, and a Federal High Court rule is not a
Lagos State rule on the same subject.

## 1. Constitution, federal legislation and regulation (official or official-adjacent)

| Source | Covers | Access | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended) | supreme law; fundamental rights; jurisdiction of courts | official text via the National Assembly and the Federal Ministry of Justice | `ng-federal` |
| Laws of the Federation of Nigeria (LFN) 2004 and later Acts | codified federal statutes | NASS website (`nass.gov.ng`) for Acts and bills; consolidated LFN texts are patchy online | `ng-nass` |
| Official Gazette of the Federal Republic | commencement, subsidiary legislation, notices | Federal Government Printer; limited online coverage | `ng-federal` |
| Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023; NDPC regulations and guidance | data protection | `ndpc.gov.ng` | `ng-ndpc` |
| Immigration Act 2015; Immigration Regulations 2017; NIS visa policy | immigration, visas, residence permits, citizenship by registration | `immigration.gov.ng` (NIS), Federal Ministry of Interior | `ng-nis`, `ng-interior` |

## 2. Courts and procedure

| Source | Covers | Access | Taxonomy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supreme Court of Nigeria judgments | binding on all courts | `supremecourt.gov.ng`; NigeriaLII | `ng-sc` |
| Court of Appeal judgments | binding on lower courts in all divisions | `courtofappeal.gov.ng`; NigeriaLII | `ng-ca` |
| Federal High Court (Civil Procedure) Rules 2019 and judgments | federal civil procedure | `fhc.gov.ng` | `ng-fhc` |
| National Industrial Court rules and judgments | employment and labour | `nicn.gov.ng` | `ng-nic` |
| High Court of Lagos State (Civil Procedure) Rules 2019; judgments | Lagos civil procedure | Lagos State Judiciary (`lagosjudiciary.gov.ng`) | `ng-lagos-high-court` |
| Lagos State Magistrates' Courts (Civil Procedure) Rules 2009 | Lagos magistrates' procedure | Lagos State Judiciary; the demo corpus holds the 2009 text | `ng-lagos-magistrates` |

## 3. Free discovery sources

- **NigeriaLII** (`nigerialii.org`): the broadest free collection of judgments and some
  legislation; a discovery source, not the official report.
- **Nigerian Weekly Law Reports (NWLR)** and other law reports: the citations courts
  expect (`(2020) 5 NWLR (Pt. 1717) 123`), available through licensed databases.

## 4. Licensed databases (retrieve on demand only; never store full text)

- **LawPavilion**, **Legalpedia**, **vLex Nigeria**: judgments, legislation, citators.
  Licence terms decide what may be cached; the source registry records them.

## Verification requirements

- Cite the law in force on the material date; check commencement and amendment.
- Distinguish ratio from obiter, and a Supreme Court decision from a Court of Appeal one.
- Citator status (whether a decision was overruled) is unavailable from free sources;
  say so rather than assume.
- A Federal High Court rule and a Lagos State rule are never interchangeable.

## Preferred sequence

Official text or judgment → NigeriaLII or the court website → licensed database →
secondary commentary. Delete any authority that cannot be verified rather than guessing
its citation.
