# Nigeria: operating rules

1. **Resolve the jurisdiction first.** Federal or state; which court or regulator; the
   material date. If the state is unknown and matters, stop and ask.
2. **Rules of court are jurisdiction-specific.** The Federal High Court rules, the Lagos
   High Court rules and the Lagos Magistrates' Courts rules each govern their own court.
   Cite the one the matter is actually in.
3. **Statute states the law; judgments interpret it.** Never present a judgment's
   reasoning as the text of a rule or section, or the reverse.
4. **Evidence and authority stay separate.** Case facts come only from the matter's
   documents; legal propositions only from authority (AGENTS.md rules 4 and 5).
5. **Locators.** Rules of court: `Order N Rule M`, with sub-rules as pinpoints
   (`Order 5 Rule 3(6)`). Acts: `Section N(x)`. Judgments: `Paragraph N` of the retrieved
   text; report citations (`NWLR`) only when the source carries them.
6. **Currency.** Say which version of a rule or Act applies and whether later amendment
   has been checked. If it has not, say "not checked for later amendment".
7. **Languages.** English is the language of the law; a French working translation is a
   reading aid, and the citation always points at the English original.
8. **Residency.** Client documents hosted outside Nigeria are a decision under the
   Nigeria Data Protection Act that the firm takes, not the software.
